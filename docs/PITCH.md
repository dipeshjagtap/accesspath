# AccessPath AI — 3-minute pitch

**Tagline:** From unmet need to delivered support

## One-liner

AccessPath helps people navigate from an assistive-technology need to a
verified next step, while helping organizations coordinate referrals and
measure whether support was actually delivered.

## Problem (0:00–0:30)

Families and caregivers often do not know where to start for mobility
support. Information is fragmented across clinics, NGOs, suppliers, and
schemes. Organizations still track follow-ups in spreadsheets and chats.

## Solution (0:30–1:40)

1. Guided mobility-support intake (no diagnosis required)
2. Confirmable needs summary
3. Grounded navigation plan using **Gemini on Vertex AI** (with
   deterministic fallback)
4. Verified resource directory with source + last-verified labels
5. Action checklist the user can update

**Guardrails:** AccessPath does not diagnose, prescribe, invent providers,
or decide scheme eligibility. Facts come from curated records only.

## Trust & evidence (1:40–2:10)

Open a provider and a program. Show verification status, source notes, and
what happens when there is no verified match.

## Business / workflow (2:10–3:00)

User consents to share selected fields with a partner organization.
Partner staff update case status. Outcomes are tracked — not just clicks.

**Who pays:** NGOs, rehab providers, hospitals, and disability-support
organizations pay for case coordination and reporting. Basic guidance can
stay free during the pilot.

## Ask

Pilot with 2–3 partners in one region (Pune demo path today), measure
referral contact/completion, and validate willingness to pay.
